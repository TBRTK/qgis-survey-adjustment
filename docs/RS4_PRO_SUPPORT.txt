EMLID REACH RS4 PRO — SUPPORT STATUS, SURVEY ADJUSTMENT 0.12.0

Full device support is NOT claimed. The plugin is receiver independent and
processes documented exports, not a direct connection to the receiver. It does
not implement receiver firmware, radios, camera/visual survey, IMU tilt or AR.
Processed coordinate/CSV review and raw RINEX processing are different workflows.
Final exported ground heights must not have antenna height subtracted again.

Emlid recommends RINEX 3.03 for RS4/RS4 Pro logging when the required version is
unknown. This is within the existing 2.11/3.02-3.05 parser profile. Format support
does not prove actual device/header/signal/antenna correctness; user observations
and independent control are needed for receiver-specific end-to-end validation.
Firmware version alone is not such validation. UBX, NMEA and RTCM are not raw
input formats in this profile; an external documented conversion may be needed.

The official NGS catalogue reviewed on 2026-10-20 lists distinct models
EML_REACH_RS4 NONE and EML_REACH_RS4P NONE. They must never be substituted.
The RS4P model has DAZI=5 and G01/G02/G05, E01/E05, R01/R02, C01/C06 plus
other constellation frequency records. The tested SA1 profile accepts DAZI=0
and G01/G02, E01/E07, C02/C07, R01/R02. Therefore the official RS4 Pro model
cannot be accepted unchanged for complete four-system calibration in 0.12.0.
It is deliberately rejected rather than silently dropping azimuth variation,
borrowing GPS patterns, relabelling C01/C02, or substituting E5a for E5b.
Azimuth-aware calibration, additional reviewed band selection and unambiguous
legacy BeiDou ANTEX frequency conventions are further work. Do not edit the
official model merely to bypass these checks. A reduced model is not full support.

Emlid distinguishes pole/mark-to-ARP height from ARP-to-phase-centre offset.
Its Flow app applies a receiver-dependent offset, while the published Studio
workflow currently describes manual handling. That Studio-specific 95 mm example
must not become a universal addition in this plugin. Review actual RINEX H/E/N,
antenna name and whether height already contains PCO. The plugin applies H/E/N
and exact reviewed frequency-specific PCO/PCV separately. Ambiguous endpoints
must be documented/resolved using preserved originals before processing.

For device validation: preserve paired base/rover OBS, matching NAV, firmware and
logging/export settings, exact antenna/radome/header, pole/ARP evidence, session
times/UTC offset, reference frame/epoch, independently known base and check point.
Keep real data and calibration files private. The included/generated examples are
invented software tests; they cannot establish accuracy or full RS4 Pro support.
