Survey Adjustment 0.2.2 – English interface, help and reports

- All plugin-owned menus, tabs, field help, validation errors and status
  messages are in English, including SALSA result and warning explanations.
- Repeatability reports and generated map layer names/fields are in English.
  Report files: check.json, check.txt, check.html, means.csv, residuals.csv.
- Output status values: WITHIN TOLERANCE, OUTSIDE TOLERANCE, INCONCLUSIVE.
  SALSA output assessment: REVIEW REQUIRED. Existing results are not rewritten.
- Root and packaged README, metadata and NOTICE are in English.
- Norwegian source-column aliases remain supported. Source observations,
  units, covariance, coordinate calculation and height treatment are preserved.
- Author, project link, GPL licence and optional support footer remain included.

Validation: all 26 tests pass in the QGIS 4.0.0 environment, including actual
SALSA runs, English report files/statuses and preservation of PointZ results.

This is an experimental prototype. Real receiver exports still need validation.
No raw GNSS/RINEX or measured-baseline processing, geoid/datum/epoch conversion
