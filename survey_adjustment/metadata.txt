Separate delivery review requires frame/epoch/height evidence, distinguishes NN2000:2018/2025, supports explicit same-frame UTM/NTM projection and reviewed local HREF height conversion. Unchanged calculations, original/adjusted/delivered PointZ coordinates, reports and checksums are preserved locally. Raw RINEX catalogue, metadata review and local map/timeline preflight share the existing network/import workflow. Explicit local GPS/Galileo/BeiDou PPK/static uses separately installed RTKLIB EX 2.5.1. Legacy upstream ANTEX remains GPS-only. Separate experimental Survey SA1 adds GLONASS float and independent G/E/C/R receiver ANTEX arrays with explicit application audit. Survey SA2 adds complete azimuth grids and explicit fixed level antenna orientations, E1/E5a and B1C/B3I bands, matching signals and independently checked correction audit. Provider copied/unverified notes remain visible. Local ETC review and explicit coordinate-only tilt compensation preserve already-compensated points; exact UTC/height/endpoint checks. Full dynamic raw tilt/yaw and full RS4 Pro device support are not claimed. No VRS, ITRF/WGS84 frame shift, epoch transformation or output uncertainty propagation. No professional delivery approval or official endorsement.
